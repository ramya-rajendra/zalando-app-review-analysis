# Insight notes

Things I noticed while tagging, to use later in the deck.

- Half of the app complaints are design, not bugs (97 of 189 in parts 1–4).
- Users link the removed product ratings directly to sizing problems.
- An update made the app incompatible with older phones and tablets (part 7, rows 59, 68, 75, 76, 81, 83).
- Top feature requests so far: dark mode, choosing DHL instead of Hermes, seeing invoice or payment status.
- Product ratings were removed and later came back (part 4, rows 139 and 198), which could make a timeline chart.

## What the retention data shows

- Very few users come back: on average 4.1% return after 1 week, 1.8% after 2 weeks and 1.1% after 4 weeks.
- Early November users came back more (6–7% in week 1) than users from December and January (3–4%). That’s the holiday season: people who arrive around Black Friday and Christmas often buy one gift and never return.
- he last rows (late January) look low partly because the data ends on 31 January, so those users had less time to return.

## story so far

- Reviews: the app (bugs and redesign) and removed product ratings frustrate users most, and payment/invoice problems keep coming back.
- Funnel: about 55% of users who start checkout don’t buy, on every device.
- Retention: most users don’t return, so losing them at checkout is expensive.


Complaint volume fell, but the underlying gap remains: customers still can’t read other buyers’ experiences before buying.

Verified-buyer fit feedback: after keeping an item, buyers answer one quick question: runs small / true to size / runs large. The product page shows the result, e.g. “68% say true to size.
